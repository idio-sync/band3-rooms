How to connect

rockband.idiosynchronic.com
  1. Name the profile player 1 starts with after yourself (not blank or “User”). Both servers know you by that name alone, with no password, so pick one nobody else uses.
  2. In band3, open Settings › Online (in the launcher or in the game).
  3. Under GoCentral, turn on Leaderboards and battles and set Server to rockband.idiosynchronic.com.
  4. Under Online play, turn on Play online and Rooms, and set Rooms server to rockband.idiosynchronic.com. Test connection shows your ping to it.
  5. Restart band3, or press Play in the launcher.

Both are band3's defaults, so you may only need to turn them on. In band3.toml they're gocentral, gocentral_address, liveless, liveless_rooms and liveless_rooms_server. Rooms only introduces games: whoever hosts still needs UDP port 9103 reachable (Open the port on the router asks the router for it).
